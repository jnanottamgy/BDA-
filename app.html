Research basis

Built on recent work, applied to Indian data

Bogomolov & Plotnikov (2024), The possibilities of using Big Data technologies in processing atmospheric air pollution data. Computational Nanotechnology 11(1).

They processed station data with PySpark and compared it with Pandas. We reproduce that approach on public CPCB data, with documented cleaning and a defined benchmark setup.
